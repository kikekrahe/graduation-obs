# Rubinstein, Daniel - Philosophy of the Digital Image

> How can we make sense of this new form of visual culture that seems to envelope us in so many different ways?

> What makes something an image?

Rubinstein argues: something which has been framed, excluded from an exterior. 

## Pre-Renaissance
Before the Renaissance, the image acted as a mediator between *you* and *divinity.* It is a spiritual and religious vehicle. The image is political but on a society surrounded by religious discourse. The painting is an act of devotion, not made with a subject in mind.
## Renaissance
Painting as a representation, of space and social structure of the contemporary field. A (socio)political vehicle.
The renaissance painting is ideated as a window through which one looks, there should be no difference between these two.
As opposed to the framework of religion in the Middle Ages, the Renaissance operates on a framework of representation. Currency, anatomy, geography, research all find themselves operating through a certain representation of reality. Politics also becomes a matter of representation. 
While the Middle Ages acted on a principle of diffused and served knowledge, the Renaissance focuses on human Enlightenment; the individual human ability to see and experience for themselves. Learn how to represent, how to frame, the world for yourself.
The frame switches from divine to human ability. 
Additionally, the painting becomes something which is consumable and made with a certain subject or viewer in mind. The human eye is put in the centre instead of that of god.

Our current ability to operate through representation relies on resemblance. Much of our world is built upon representations of things, semiotics plague our walls and only resemblance allows us to make sense of it. Resemblance is, in turn, a logical operation based on judgement, an action that calls in the necessity for a 'judge', someone who is fit and trusted to conduct this operation. With this, a political structure is erected.

The weight carried by photography as something which represents truth is because it is believed to be rationally made imagery. Its truth spawns from this power of rational representational. Representation, our act of seeing the world for ourselves, is a way of making images based on rationality.
