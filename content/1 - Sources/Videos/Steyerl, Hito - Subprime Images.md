# Steyerl, Hito - Subprime Images

**Model Collapse** is one of the consequences of AI image generation, a result of models being trained on their own outputs and leading to a degenerative learning process. The model starts forgetting improbable events over time as the model becomes poisoned with its own projection of reality. 
Image generation operates on the collective and collaborative creation of said image; its product relying on a large set of participants such as ghosts workers who label images, curators who filter them, those who lay down the technical feats and the infrastructure etc etc. The resulting image, however, is then privatised in the hands of the creator.
