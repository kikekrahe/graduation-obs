# Rubinstein, Daniel - 2. Heidegger Age of the World Picture

The introduction of the photograph allowed painting to be released of the burden of representation. Instead photography took on this weight. The networked, digital landscape instead allows photography to be released of this, given that representational images are to be found rather than to be made; they already exist so why make more?
With the introduction or representation in photography, the image as a functional object, its origins and authors become secondary. 
The representational burden lifted off of painting allowed painting to become less about what the object looks like and more about what looking looks like. 

Hegel says: 
> What is rational is real, and what is real is rational

Heidegger proposes that science deals with a variety of knowledges which are presupposed to be truthful and established. It operates within a certain path of truthfulness and doesn't fall outside of it; it deals not with mythology, likings, death, ghosts etc. 
Art on the other hand, after being released of the burden of representation, deals precisely with this. 

If anthropology is the making sense of the world by putting the human in the center, from the human standpoint; it can be argued that photography is the same thing.
