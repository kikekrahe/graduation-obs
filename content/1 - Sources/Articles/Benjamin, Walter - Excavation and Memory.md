# Benjamin, Walter - Excavation and Memory

> He who seeks to approach his own buried past must conduct himself like a man digging.

Memory is not considered a tool through which to access the past but rather the medium in which the past is buried. The process of excavating and unearthing this memory is one that should be fragmented, sporadic and messy, as opposed to traditional forms of historiography. The goal of this is not only to come to a specific artefact but rather to analyse the path needed to reach it; to uncover dominant narratives and challenge them.
