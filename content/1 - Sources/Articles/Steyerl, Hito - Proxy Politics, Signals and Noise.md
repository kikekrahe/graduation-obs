# Steyerl, Hito - Proxy Politics, Signals and Noise

> [The Algorithm] creates the present picture based on earlier pictures, on your/its memory. This new paradigm is being called [[computational photography]].

Although Steyerl refers to the algorithmic actions of the digital camera when assembling a low-resolution, noisy image, this text is representative of the work carried out by AI models in generative image making. I especially like the idea of computer memory as something used to fill in space.

> This type of photography is speculative and relational. It is a gamble with probability that bets with inertia. It makes seeing unforeseen things more difficult.

AI Image Generation gives way to the probable and hides the improbable in its speculative image generation.

> The camera turns into a social projector rather than a recorder. It shows a superposition of what it thinks you might want to look like plus what others think you should buy or be. 

Images spewed out by AI are not so much a direct representation of your prompt but rather a social projector which projects western society's aesthetic, political and commercial values.

> An image becomes less of a representation than a proxy, a mercenary of appearance, a floating texture-surface-commodity.

When images, or sections of images, are used in the generation of a new one, their dissociation with their origin and original make the resulting image less of a representation rather than a collage.
